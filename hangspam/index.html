HangSpam

A hangman word game for the whole class on one screen. The teacher types a secret word, the class calls out letters, and every wrong guess lowers a worried snack closer to a sizzling pan.

Your word, or your lists

The free game needs no account: the teacher types any word or short phrase, hidden until Show. A teacher with a Class Cookies subscription can also play through their own word lists from My Classroom, one word after another.

The snack is only ever cooked in a cartoon way, and there is no noose: it hangs from a rolling pin. HangSpam was built on 21 October 2026 from a design handed over beforehand: five commits on the production branch, each with a Claude co-author line. It reads a teacher's lists with the teacher's own sign-in and never writes to the platform.

Checked, 21 October 2026

127 automated tests passing; typecheck and lint with no errors. Like Spaghetti Smash, every screen is laid out on a fixed stage and scaled to fit the window, so the page itself never scrolls. Phones get a note asking for a bigger screen.
