Spaghetti Smash

A whack-a-mole word game for teams on one screen. Food characters pop up from bowls holding word signs; one student per turn smashes the right words with a frying pan before they hide.

Play Spaghetti Smash ↗

Free lists, or your own

Everyone can play the three free lists with no account. A teacher with a Class Cookies subscription can play their own word lists from My Classroom instead, and choose which free list supplies the wrong words.

Spaghetti Smash was built on 19 October 2026 from a design handed over beforehand: ten commits on the production branch, each with a Claude co-author line. The game reads a teacher's lists with the teacher's own sign-in and never writes to the platform.

Checked, 19 October 2026

203 automated tests passing; typecheck and lint with no errors. Every screen is laid out for a classroom TV and scaled to fit the window, so nothing a class sees ever scrolls.
